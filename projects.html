Financial Performance Dashboard & Analysis

A structured financial analysis project built from a 700+ row transactional dataset, translated into a Power BI reporting concept that surfaces revenue, cost, profit, and margin insights at a glance.

Revenue

Problem

The business needed a clearer view of profitability across products and market segments. Metrics were available but the reporting flow was fragmented and hard to interpret quickly. Decision-makers lacked a single source of truth for performance review.

Methodology

I cleaned and structured a 700+ row dataset, standardized all fields, and built pivot-based analysis in Excel. I then designed a dashboard framework presenting revenue, costs, profit, and margin in a digestible format — ready for Power BI implementation.

Impact & Results

The project transformed raw financial data into a more understandable and actionable reporting view. By aligning the analysis around revenue, cost, profit, and margin, it became easier to communicate performance and identify where the business was strongest across its product categories.
