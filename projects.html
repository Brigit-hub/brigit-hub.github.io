🎯 Problem

The business needed a clearer view of profitability across products and market segments. Metrics were available but the reporting flow was fragmented and hard to interpret quickly.

⚙️ Methodology

Cleaned and structured 700+ rows, standardized fields, built pivot-based analysis in Excel, and designed a dashboard framework ready for Power BI implementation.

The actual analysis — built in Excel & Power BI

Sales overview — revenue by category, profit trends, YoY comparison
Regional performance — state filters, regional breakdowns, geographic comparison
Product analysis — subcategory filters, YoY comparison, quantity distribution
Executive summary — KPIs, profit margin analysis, segment breakdown

Impact & Results

📊 700+ rows

📦 4 categories

💰 14.2% margin

Clear visibility into which segments drove the most business value.
