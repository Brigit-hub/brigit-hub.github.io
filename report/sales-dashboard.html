Key Insights

Office Supplies Dominates

Office Supplies generates $67M in sales with a 26.69% profit margin, making it the strongest category with consistent quarterly performance.

Action: Maintain current go-to-market strategy for Office Supplies. Use this category's success model for launching new products in weaker categories.

Furniture & Technology Close Behind

Furniture ($27M) and Technology ($17M) are secondary revenue drivers. Technology shows higher growth potential (+22% YoY), while Furniture offers more stable margins.

Action: Develop premium furniture bundles to increase average deal size. Accelerate technology sales through targeted digital campaigns.

Profit Margin Analysis

Office Supplies maintains strongest margins (26.69%), followed by Technology (26.57%) and Furniture (26.49%). Consistency suggests healthy pricing strategy across categories.

Action: Investigate why Technology shows higher YoY growth despite similar margins. Replicate success factors in other categories.

Seasonal Sales Patterns

Sales peak in October/December with ~$11M+ monthly revenue, dropping to $6-7M in slower months. Year-over-year comparison shows consistent patterns between 2022-2024.

Action: Schedule promotional campaigns in March, May, and August. Reserve 40% of annual budget for Q4. Pre-order campaigns in July/August for holiday season.

Allocate 50% budget to Office Supplies, 30% to Technology, 20% to Furniture based on revenue contribution.
