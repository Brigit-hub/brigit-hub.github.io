Product Insights

Chairs Dominate Furniture Category

Chairs generate $31.2M in sales with exceptional 124.68% YoY growth, making it the clear star product.

Action: Invest heavily in Chairs production. Develop premium Chairs line. Expand to new geographic markets. Create bundle offers with Desks for home office solutions.

Laptops Second Highest Growth

Laptops show $30.5M in sales with strong 122.54% YoY growth, placing them as the second-best performer.

Action: Increase Laptop inventory 50% to capture holiday season demand. Partner with tech vendors for bundled offerings.

Phones Emerging Opportunity

Phones generate $29.8M in sales with 93.60% YoY growth, indicating emerging market demand.

Action: Test premium phone tier. Create "office mobility" bundles. Investigate why growth trails Chairs/Laptops.

Paper Product Weakness

Paper shows only 4.84% YoY sales growth and declining market share. While generating ~$10M annually, this product line faces margin pressure.

Action: Consider Paper product discontinuation or repositioning. Redirect sales focus to high-growth categories.

Accessories & Support Items

Accessories (Binders, Pens, Phones, Bookcases) represent $10-15M each in sales with moderate growth (62-86% YoY).

Action: Use accessories as margin-expansion products bundled with high-ticket items. Focus on impulse buying in e-commerce.
