📊 Product Insights

Chairs Dominate Furniture Category

Chairs generate $31.2M in sales with exceptional 124.68% YoY growth, making it the clear star product. This subcategory is outpacing all competitors by significant margin.

💡 Action: Invest heavily in Chairs production to meet surging demand. Develop premium Chairs line. Expand Chairs to new geographic markets (Mexico). Create Chairs bundle offers with Desks for home office solutions.

Laptops Second Highest Growth

Laptops show $30.5M in sales with strong 122.54% YoY growth, placing them as the second-best performer. Technology category is gaining momentum alongside traditional furniture.

💡 Action: Increase Laptop inventory 50% to capture holiday season demand. Partner with tech vendors for bundled offerings. Launch tech-savvy marketing campaign targeting remote workers.

Phones Emerging Opportunity

Phones generate $29.8M in sales with 93.60% YoY growth, indicating emerging market demand. While growing, penetration is still below peak performers.

💡 Action: Test premium phone tier. Develop mobile-first marketing campaign. Create "office mobility" bundles (Phones + Chairs + Desks). Investigate why growth trails Chairs/Laptops.

Paper Product Weakness

Paper shows only 4.84% YoY sales growth and declining market share. While still generating ~$10M annually, this product line faces margin pressure and declining demand.

💡 Action: Consider Paper product discontinuation or significant repositioning. Redirect sales team focus to high-growth categories. Negotiate bulk purchasing discounts to maintain margins on remaining Paper sales.

Accessories & Support Items

Accessories (Binders, Pens, Phones, Bookcases) represent $10-15M each in sales with moderate growth (62-86% YoY). These support products provide stable revenue base.

💡 Action: Use accessories as margin-expansion products bundled with high-ticket items. Focus on impulse buying in e-commerce. Create tiered pricing for bulk office buyers.
