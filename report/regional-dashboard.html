📊 Geographic Insights

Ohio Leads All States

Ohio generates $12.7M in revenue with solid $1.8M profit (14.2% margin). Illinois follows at $11.8M. These two states are consistently strong performers.

💡 Action: Designate Ohio & Illinois as Tier-1 regions. Assign senior sales resources. Develop localized marketing campaigns. Consider opening satellite offices to increase coverage.

Central Region Strength

The Central region (Ohio, Illinois, Michigan, Pennsylvania) dominates with 42% of total sales ($50.4M) and highest quantity sold. This is your strongest market concentration.

💡 Action: Protect Central region with dedicated account managers. Develop deep customer relationships. Create regional customer advisory board for feedback.

East Region Steady Performer

Eastern states (Florida, New York, Pennsylvania) generate ~$38.8M in sales with balanced performance. California adds $13M, showing strong West coast presence but less developed than East.

💡 Action: Maintain current East region resource allocation. Increase California investment as second priority after Central region expansion.

Texas Growth Opportunity

Texas underperforms at $11M despite being the 2nd largest state by population. This represents either market penetration opportunity or competitive weakness that needs investigation.

💡 Action: Conduct competitive analysis in Texas. Schedule customer interviews to understand barriers. Consider launching Texas-specific promotional campaign with aggressive pricing. Hire regional sales manager.

Mexico & International Potential

Mexico shows very low sales, indicating either limited market development or barriers to entry. International markets represent untapped growth potential.

💡 Action: Develop Mexico market entry strategy. Research regulatory/tariff issues. Consider partnerships with local distributors. Pilot Spanish-language marketing in Q2 2026.
