Business Understanding

Context & Importance

Organizations rely on financial data analytics to monitor performance, manage costs, and support strategic budgeting decisions. In this project, financial sales data was analyzed to identify patterns and trends related to revenue generation, expenses, and profitability.

Problem Statement

The company needs to determine which products and regions generate the highest revenue and profit while identifying areas where operational costs can be reduced to improve overall financial performance.

Key Business Questions

  • Which product generates the highest revenue?
  • What are the total revenues, expenses, and profits across product categories and regions?

Section 03

Data Preparation

Several data cleaning steps were performed to ensure accuracy, consistency, and readiness for analysis:

✓Duplicate Check: Verified for duplicate records — none found
✓Text Standardization: Standardized all text values using PROPER() function for consistency across categorical columns
✓Outlier Removal: Identified and removed row 11 which contained multiple missing dependent values
✓Missing Values (Categorical): Replaced missing categorical values with the mode in their respective columns
✓Outlier Analysis: Used descriptive statistics to identify outliers and handled appropriately
✓Missing Values (Numerical): Replaced missing numerical values with the median where appropriate
✓Calculated Fields: Derived missing Sales values using formula: Sales = Gross Sales − Discounts
✓New Metrics: Created Profit Margin column using formula: Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Sales
Data Quality Result:

Dataset is now clean, consistent, and ready for modeling with no missing critical values and all calculations validated.

Section 04

Data Modeling & Key Insights

Pivot tables and pivot charts were created to summarize financial performance across products, regions, segments, discount levels, and time trends.

Product Performance Analysis

Paseo is the clear revenue leader at approximately $33M, followed by VTT (~$24M) and Velo (~$22M). Carretera and Amarita generate significantly lower revenue, presenting either repositioning opportunities or product discontinuation options.

Example Implementation: Focus 60% of marketing budget on Paseo while running a 90-day pilot program for Carretera repositioning in emerging markets.

Regional Performance Analysis

The United States dominates with ~$25M in sales, followed by Canada (~$24.3M), France (~$23.4M), and Germany (~$23.4M). Mexico generates only ~$2.9M, representing significant untapped potential.

Example Implementation: Launch a dedicated Mexico expansion initiative with localized pricing and partnerships with local distributors.

Customer Segment Analysis

Government segment dominates with 63% of profits (~$10.6M), while Enterprise contributes 23%, Midmarket 7%, Channel Partners 4%, and Small Business 3%.

Example Implementation: Create a dedicated Government Solutions team with compliance expertise (FISMA, FedRAMP).

Discount Impact Analysis

High-Impact Finding: Products with no discounts maintain 28.99% profit margins, while high discounts reduce margins to below 26%. Every 10% discount increase reduces profitability by 2-3 percentage points.

Example Implementation: Implement a discount governance policy: Max 5% for Government, 10% for Enterprise, 15% for SMB.

Seasonal Trends & Monthly Patterns

Sales exhibit strong seasonality with peaks in Q4 (Oct ~$11.2M, Dec ~$11M, Nov ~$10.1M, Sep ~$9.8M) and valleys in Q2 (Mar ~$6.8M, May ~$6.7M, Aug ~$7.1M).

Example Implementation: Allocate 40% of annual marketing budget to Q4, hire seasonal sales staff in August, launch flash promotions in March/May/August.
