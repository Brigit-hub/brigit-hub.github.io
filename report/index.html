Business Understanding

Context & Importance

Organizations rely on financial data analytics to monitor performance, manage costs, and support strategic budgeting decisions. In this project, financial sales data was analyzed to identify patterns and trends related to revenue generation, expenses, and profitability.

Problem Statement

The company needs to determine which products and regions generate the highest revenue and profit while identifying areas where operational costs can be reduced to improve overall financial performance.

Section 2

Data Understanding

The dataset used in this analysis is the Microsoft Financial Sample dataset, which contains financial sales information across multiple countries and product categories.

Key Variables in Dataset

Segment Customer type (Government, Enterprise, SMB, etc.)
Country Sales region and geographic markets
Product Product categories (Paseo, VTT, Velo, Carretera, etc.)
Discount Band Pricing tier and discount levels
Units Sold Quantity of units per transaction
Manufacturing Price Cost per unit to manufacture
Sale Price Price per unit to customer
Gross Sales Revenue before discounts and COGS
Discounts Discount amounts applied
Sales Net sales after discounts
COGS Cost of Goods Sold
Profit Sales minus COGS
Profit Margin Calculated as Profit ÷ Sales
Date & Year Transaction date for temporal analysis

Section 3

Data Preparation

Several data cleaning steps were performed to ensure accuracy, consistency, and readiness for analysis:

✓ Duplicate Check: Verified for duplicate records — none found
✓ Text Standardization: Standardized all text values using PROPER() function for consistency across categorical columns
✓ Outlier Removal: Identified and removed row 11 which contained multiple missing dependent values
✓ Missing Values (Categorical): Replaced missing categorical values with the mode (most frequent value) in their respective columns
✓ Outlier Analysis: Used descriptive statistics to identify outliers and handled appropriately
✓ Missing Values (Numerical): Replaced missing numerical values with the median where appropriate
✓ Calculated Fields: Derived missing Sales values using formula: Sales = Gross Sales − Discounts
✓ New Metrics: Created Profit Margin column using formula: Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Sales
Data Quality Result:

Dataset is now clean, consistent, and ready for modeling with no missing critical values and all calculations validated.

Section 4

Data Modeling & Key Insights

Pivot tables and pivot charts were created to summarize financial performance across products, regions, segments, discount levels, and time trends.

📊 Product Performance Analysis

Paseo is the clear revenue leader at approximately $33M, followed by VTT (~$24M) and Velo (~$22M). Carretera and Amarita generate significantly lower revenue, presenting either repositioning opportunities or product discontinuation options.

📌 Example Implementation: Focus 60% of marketing budget on Paseo while running a 90-day pilot program for Carretera repositioning in emerging markets. Reallocate underperforming sales resources.

🌍 Regional Performance Analysis

The United States dominates with ~$25M in sales, followed by Canada (~$24.3M), France (~$23.4M), and Germany (~$23.4M). Mexico generates only ~$2.9M, representing significant untapped potential or barriers to growth.

📌 Example Implementation: Launch a dedicated Mexico expansion initiative with localized pricing, partnerships with local distributors, and Spanish-language support. Benchmark against similar markets.

👥 Customer Segment Analysis

Government segment dominates with 63% of profits (~$10.6M), while Enterprise contributes 23%, Midmarket 7%, Channel Partners 4%, and Small Business 3%. This shows strong public sector affinity.

📌 Example Implementation: Create a dedicated Government Solutions team with compliance expertise (FISMA, FedRAMP). Develop tiered pricing for Enterprise to improve their 20% profit margin to align with Government segment performance.

💰 Discount Impact Analysis

High-Impact Finding: Products with no discounts maintain 28.99% profit margins, while high discounts reduce margins to below 26%. Every 10% discount increase reduces profitability by 2-3 percentage points.

📌 Example Implementation: Implement a discount governance policy: Max 5% for Government, 10% for Enterprise, 15% for SMB. Use volume-based tiering instead of percentage discounts. Track all discounts in a centralized approval system.

📅 Seasonal Trends & Monthly Patterns

Sales exhibit strong seasonality with peaks in Q4 (Oct ~$11.2M, Dec ~$11M, Nov ~$10.1M, Sep ~$9.8M) and valleys in Q2 (Mar ~$6.8M, May ~$6.7M, Aug ~$7.1M). This pattern is consistent across 2013-2014.

📌 Example Implementation: Allocate 40% of annual marketing budget to Q4, hire seasonal sales staff in August, launch flash promotions in March/May/August, plan inventory 3 months in advance based on historical patterns.
